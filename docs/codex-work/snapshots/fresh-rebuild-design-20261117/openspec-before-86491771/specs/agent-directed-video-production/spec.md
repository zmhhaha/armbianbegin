## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Agent jointly plans narration and visual expression
The workbench SHALL use a shared-Agent director role to jointly organize narration, understanding objectives, segmentation, visual mechanisms, selected and combined published creative methods, overall style, adjacent transitions and a resource plan with dependencies, expression objectives, explicitly required capabilities or key effects and execution bounds from source material. By default it MAY reorganize and make the material conversational while preserving facts, meaning and sources. An explicit unchanged-script request SHALL preserve the requested text. The protocol SHALL NOT require the pre-existing shot count, IDs or order.

#### Scenario: Source material requires a different organization
- **WHEN** an owner requests a video from material without forbidding rewriting
- **THEN** the director proposes a coherent narration and visual plan with appropriate segmentation and traceable source constraints
- **AND** an unchanged-script instruction takes precedence over optional rewriting

### Requirement: One overall plan confirmation uses existing demonstrations
The workbench SHALL show the proposed narration, understanding objectives, visual methods, applicable existing library demonstrations supporting the selected creative methods, style and transitions for one overall owner confirmation. New representative clips and per-shot static-frame approval SHALL NOT be mandatory or generated by default. Uncovered expressions SHALL identify their free-creation plan. Owner confirmation SHALL freeze applicable plan dependencies without claiming approval of ungenerated media.

#### Scenario: Owner reviews the proposal
- **WHEN** the owner opens an unconfirmed plan
- **THEN** the proposal and existing effect demonstrations can be reviewed together without consuming a new animation-generation task
- **AND** a newly generated trial is optional and requires an explicit request

### Requirement: Confirmed audio determines animation timing
After overall confirmation the shared resource orchestration SHALL produce or select the applicable narration audio. Narrated passages SHALL bind dependent animation to its actual measured duration and content identity; passages without narration SHALL use director-planned timing and MAY retain sound effects. Planning estimates SHALL describe intent without freezing measured timing before the applicable recording exists. It SHALL preserve one project voice, default to no overlay captions and label alignment sources honestly. It SHALL NOT truncate or stretch narration to conceal mismatches or represent estimated word timing as forced alignment.

#### Scenario: Create animation from confirmed narration
- **WHEN** the selected recording is valid for the confirmed plan
- **THEN** source creation receives the measured audio duration, applicable text and truthful timing evidence before it generates the final animation

#### Scenario: Alignment is estimated
- **WHEN** only estimated word timing is available
- **THEN** the system may use it for an explicitly labelled draft while preserving the actual total audio duration and pending human timing review

#### Scenario: A passage has no explanatory narration
- **WHEN** the confirmed expression plan contains a non-narrated demonstration
- **THEN** the director defines its timing under the same whole-film clock and applicable sound effects remain available
- **AND** the workflow does not invent a silent narration recording merely to determine its duration

### Requirement: Whole-video context survives segmented execution
The orchestration SHALL retain global narration, style, subject identity, adjacent start and end states and transition intent when splitting work into segment tasks. Agents SHALL be able to select and combine applicable published creative methods and freely author generated source within admitted runtimes. Actual method text, versions, applicability and delivered input provenance SHALL be bound to the candidate; reading supporting source is optional and SHALL NOT substitute for method delivery. New publications SHALL NOT silently change frozen method inputs. Resumable execution, cancellation, frozen history and late-result protection SHALL apply. Internal draft candidate selection SHALL be distinct from owner adoption, aesthetic acceptance and human timing verification.

#### Scenario: Generate a multi-segment draft
- **WHEN** the owner confirms one overall plan
- **THEN** the system can create audio, generate context-aware segments and assemble a draft without mandatory per-shot adoption clicks
- **AND** it does not forge owner adoption or human timing approval to achieve that automation

### Requirement: Independent review observes actual rendered evidence
A distinct shared-Agent reviewer role SHALL receive evidence from the actual rendered candidate, with criteria and media identities, applicable ordered temporal evidence, observation coverage and limitations, and actual sound evidence when required by the objective. Findings SHALL identify the affected version, interval, visible evidence, issue and requested change. Source inspection and creator assertions SHALL NOT substitute for observing media. Models, profiles and actual multimodal transport SHALL be verified before claiming the role can review motion; audio or full-video understanding SHALL NOT be claimed without applicable observed input.

#### Scenario: Reviewer identifies a visible failure
- **WHEN** a rendered candidate contains a known visible issue within the supplied observation window
- **THEN** the reviewer can return a bound time range and actionable visual finding supported by supplied media
- **AND** the result remains separate from technical rendering validation and owner approval

#### Scenario: Required media cannot be observed
- **WHEN** access, transport or model capability prevents the required observation
- **THEN** review is reported as incomplete or unsupported instead of successful
- **AND** unseen content and unheard audio receive no fabricated quality judgement

### Requirement: Runtime admission is separate from creative freedom
Generated HTML/SVG, Canvas and Three.js SHALL each use an explicitly admitted isolated runtime with time seeking, applicable deterministic state and randomness, frozen dependencies, resource bounds and preview/export evidence. Creative expression SHALL NOT be limited to developer-authored composition templates. Existing compiler paths and frozen outputs SHALL retain their reconstruction meaning; freely generated cross-segment continuity SHALL require its own actual boundary evidence rather than inheriting fixed-compiler approval.

#### Scenario: Admit a new generated-source engine
- **WHEN** Canvas or Three.js is proposed for production-generated source
- **THEN** its actual execution, reverse and forward positioning, resource and MP4 consistency evidence must pass independently before effects requiring it are production-available
- **AND** removing a guard or playing a fixed demonstration alone does not satisfy admission

#### Scenario: Claim cross-segment continuous movement
- **WHEN** a project advertises continuous subject movement across generated segments
- **THEN** actual subject identity and boundary motion are evaluated in the rendered composition separately from ordinary concatenation or common styling

### Requirement: Sound effects are separate from explanatory narration
The workbench SHALL support traceable sound-effect assets or bounded synthesis results, time-bound events and actual mixed output under the shared animation clock. Production SHALL preserve applicable narration and its actual timing while adding selected effects; laboratory demonstrations MAY omit narration without losing their intended effects. Preview and export SHALL share the applicable event schedule and validated media identities. Sound observation and human listening SHALL be recorded separately from visual or technical checks.

#### Scenario: A published method uses a sound-supported transition
- **WHEN** production selects the method for an applicable task
- **THEN** its sound events can be rendered and mixed with the project's narration without truncating that narration
- **AND** absence of applicable sound-observation evidence remains an explicit quality limitation

### Requirement: HyperFrames is the unified project and rendering foundation
Both laboratory demonstrations and formal Agent-authored production SHALL use HyperFrames as the shared animation-project, timeline, preview-seeking and rendering foundation. The developer-facing visual and animation creation category SHALL include HTML/CSS/SVG, Canvas2D, Three.js and Manim. HTML/CSS/SVG, Canvas2D and Three.js/WebGL SHALL be admitted browser drawing capabilities within that foundation; Agent-authored Manim SHALL use its separately admitted offline drawing and animation resource path without requiring GSAP or Anime.js; GSAP and Anime.js MAY provide compatible timeline and tween authoring in the same authoring layer; neither SHALL be advertised as a drawing engine or a separate public service. HyperFrames SHALL NOT be represented merely as an optional final converter for an independently defined custom animation format. Generic clock, resource, readiness and isolation adapters MAY be implemented without fixing visual compositions or action recipes. Rendering SHALL remain in video-service for this scope rather than requiring a new public renderer or separate Canvas/Three services.

#### Scenario: A laboratory effect uses Canvas or Three.js
- **WHEN** an admitted Agent-generated effect is previewed or encoded
- **THEN** the actual HyperFrames runtime drives its project time and ready frames
- **AND** repeated, reverse and random seeking, asynchronous readiness, reproducibility, resource use and real encoded output are independently verified for that engine
- **AND** installed dependency metadata, browser playback or older custom-renderer approval alone is insufficient

### Requirement: Formal delivery is one whole-film HyperFrames project
The director SHALL coordinate narration, real audio identity and measured narration timing where present, director-planned non-narrated intervals, subjects, style, adjacent states, transitions, resources and applicable sound events in one whole-film HyperFrames project. Implementation MAY distribute work across source files, scenes, subcompositions, Agent tasks and render chunks, but every part SHALL preserve the same project identity and global-time contract. One giant HTML file or model call SHALL NOT be required. Independent rendered-video concatenation alone SHALL NOT establish this project-level integration. Preview and export SHALL resolve the same frozen source, audio and schedule.

#### Scenario: Multiple Agents author adjacent scenes
- **WHEN** production is divided into scene tasks or render chunks
- **THEN** each receives the applicable whole-film context, global placement and neighbouring-state requirements
- **AND** their outputs assemble as subparts of the exact whole-film HyperFrames project rather than unrelated final videos

#### Scenario: Existing custom-renderer evidence is reused during migration
- **WHEN** prior Canvas, Three.js or neighbouring-state checks are available
- **THEN** they retain their original limited technical meaning
- **AND** the unified HyperFrames path still requires its own runtime, media, audio and boundary evidence before it is declared complete

### Requirement: Formal production admits optional authoring libraries and generated resources separately
Formal production SHALL freeze the exact admitted authoring-library versions and resources in its run and whole-film project. Agent-authored Anime.js tracks SHALL remain paused and use the HyperFrames global clock. A requested Manim resource SHALL enter the director-planned shared resource workflow and be generated through the separately admitted source-only recipe role and isolated Cairo runtime before its dependent consumer is authored. This is one producer of shared-video-resource-production rather than a permanent module-specific orchestration branch. The module role SHALL retain its source-only privileges. Generated resources SHALL be distinct from trusted fixed libraries, bound to their authorized owner, project, task, immutable resource version and actual consumers, and checked for exact manifests, source provenance, dimensions, local frame or time intervals, aggregate budgets and real stopped collection. Resource-local timing SHALL be distinguished from its placement in the whole film; applicable global placement and consumer bindings SHALL remain frozen in the film snapshot. No independent MP4 concatenation SHALL substitute for this integration. Technical availability SHALL remain separate from developer acceptance and Skill publication.

#### Scenario: A formal Agent uses Anime.js in a new film
- **WHEN** a real production module uses an admitted Anime.js library
- **THEN** the original Agent source and exact runtime assets enter the single frozen HyperFrames film
- **AND** actual global-time positioning and encoded media are verified rather than inferred from installed dependencies or a laboratory sample

#### Scenario: A formal scene requests a Manim resource
- **WHEN** a bounded resource requirement is planned and frozen through the shared resource layer in an ordinary production run
- **THEN** the separate recipe Agent and isolated Cairo executor generate the resource in dependency order before its consuming source is authored, and the consumer receives exact usage instructions, local timing and asset hashes
- **AND** the completed resource is consumed on the existing global film clock without granting the module Python execution privileges

#### Scenario: Resource generation is interrupted or not admitted
- **WHEN** generation is cancelled, fails, has an uncertain submission result, or its producer or consumer is not admitted
- **THEN** the run reconciles the same durable task identity, rejects stale or foreign resources and does not silently submit a second paid attempt
- **AND** incompatible producer-dependent methods remain excluded until the exact required production capabilities are admitted


### Requirement: A creation batch freezes one WikiSkill release from planning onward
The system SHALL freeze the active or explicitly retained historical WikiSkill release when a new joint-planning batch begins. Planning, resource creation, drawing and refinement SHALL consume the same release. Starting production from an adopted plan SHALL inherit its release and SHALL NOT fail merely because a newer default now exists.

#### Scenario: The active release changes after planning
- **THEN** the adopted plan and its creation continue with the frozen release

#### Scenario: The owner recreates an existing project using newer skills
- **THEN** a new complete planning batch SHALL freeze the new active release, or an explicitly selected retained historical release, while previous outputs remain intact
