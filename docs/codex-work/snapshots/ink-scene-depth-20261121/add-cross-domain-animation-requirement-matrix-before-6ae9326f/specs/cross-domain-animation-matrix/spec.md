## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Diverse structured animation requirements

Each priority expression family SHALL include at least four reviewed finite cross-domain requirements with different underlying data or state transitions, audience, goal, entities, questions, expected conclusion and limitations. Each of the other five engine families SHALL have three newly designed cross-domain requirements. The active corpus SHALL contain 27 new requirements. The dedicated lab SHALL start a new baseline without retired sample identities or historical annotations, SHALL reject retired public sample routes and mounted reports, and SHALL preserve new annotations on subsequent same-baseline restarts. Business projects and media SHALL remain outside this reset. A/B variants SHALL preserve content, values, conclusions and duration.

#### Scenario: A family is evaluated beyond its original use case

- **WHEN** the developer selects a new domain requirement
- **THEN** actual input data and event structure are available with the requirement brief
- **AND** differences are not limited to a renamed title or decorative labels

### Requirement: Reproducible bounded coverage suites

The developer tool SHALL generate a seed-bound finite requirement suite with exact case, input, artifact and runtime references and module/domain/engine/complexity coverage. Preparation SHALL NOT imply execution, visual approval or production adoption. Real captures and technical outcomes SHALL retain their own identities and failures.

#### Scenario: A suite is exported and reproduced

- **WHEN** identical source, seed and selection are used
- **THEN** the suite identity and selected requirements remain reproducible
- **AND** missing or unexecuted cases remain explicitly unexecuted

### Requirement: Seekable genuine rendering and truthful integration

New interface samples SHALL use actual HTML DOM, chart/algorithm samples SHALL update their actual SVG objects, and all states SHALL derive from absolute time. Technical observations SHALL bind measured DOM, values and frames, verify action targets and reset, and retain the fixed dependency boundary. The UI SHALL expose domain/engine filters and requirement details only in the developer tool; new families SHALL remain compatible with module feedback handoff.

#### Scenario: A developer seeks backwards or changes domain

- **WHEN** a sample is reset or another requirement is selected
- **THEN** state and feedback remain bound to the selected frozen input
- **AND** technical success is not described as aesthetic approval or arbitrary production support
